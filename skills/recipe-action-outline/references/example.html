Veg plan

Week 1: broccoli, carrot, sweetcorn. Week 2: add bell pepper. Week 3: add green beans. Peas can go in any week. Sweetcorn stays in every box as the safe veg.

Plate proportions

Aim for half veg, a quarter chicken (150–180 g raw), a quarter cooled rice (120–150 g cooked). Start with about a third veg and grow it.

Batches

Sunday for Monday to Wednesday, Thursday for Thursday and Friday. Cooked chicken and rice keep about 3 days in the fridge.
