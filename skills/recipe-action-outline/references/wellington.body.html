Amounts

Illustrative, my estimate, not from a source. Scale the fillet and pastry together.

Make ahead

Outlines 1 and 2 can be done the day before. Bake straight from the fridge, add 5 min if it is very cold.

Core temperature

48 °C for medium rare after resting. Check with a probe, ovens vary.
