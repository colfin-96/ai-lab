Assemble the boxes

Per box: veg, rice and sweetcorn in one compartment, chicken in another, chili cream and toppings in small pots.

Plate proportions

Aim for half veg, a quarter chicken (150–180 g raw), a quarter cooled rice (120–150 g cooked). Amounts here are my estimate, not from a source.

Batches

Cook on Sunday for Monday to Wednesday, or on Thursday for Thursday and Friday. Cooked chicken and rice keep about 3 days in the fridge.
